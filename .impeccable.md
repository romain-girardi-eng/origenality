## Design Context

### Users
Patristics scholars, doctoral students and librarians who open the map to
ask whether a neighbourhood of Origen studies is already dense. They arrive
from a phone as often as from a desk, often mid-search, and they need the
field itself in front of them, not chrome.

### Brand Personality
Measured, exact, editorial. Three words: cream, precise, unhurried.
The voice is the same as the Method page: it says what a figure counts
and what it cannot say. No marketing register.

### Chosen Direction
- Intent: a bibliographic map that reads as a leaf of parchment, not as a
  dashboard. The first impression is the landing: Origen dissolved into
  particles, one sentence, one door. The Explorer's particle clusters are
  the thing a visitor remembers once they are inside.
- Type pairing: EB Garamond for the wordmark and titles; Literata for
  reading and controls. Both already subsetted and loaded locally.
- Palette seed: the cream field (`#EFE9DA`, `#F7F2E6`, `#E6DECB`), ink
  (`#23201B`), terracotta accent (`#A03620`), and the six language tints
  already published on the map.
- Motion language: calm, sequential, restrained. The existing
  `cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)` stay; new motion is opacity and transform
  only, and it yields to `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Signature device: the particle Origen on the landing, then the particle
  clusters on the cream field. Interface furniture folds away on a narrow
  screen so the field keeps the room.

### Moodboard
The committed look is the live site itself (direction C, August 2026).
This pass does not invent a second identity; it makes that one hold on a
375 px phone with the same severity as on a desk.

### Design Principles
1. The map is the page. Chrome is borrowed, never owned.
2. One population, one vocabulary, one voice: counts stay where they are.
3. Touch gets a 44 px floor; hover lift is for fine pointers only.
4. Safe-area insets are part of the layout, not an afterthought.
5. A closed control is out of the page (inert, hidden), not merely off-screen.
6. Observatory, Method and Credits are a folio, not a dashboard: display
   titles, one opening figure where a count is the subject, tables as a
   typeset register, no tiles.
